Cozy Chess

See the game, one calm move at a time.

Cozy Chess is a quiet chess app for learning without pressure. Start against the Rookie, win three games to face the Master, then the Grandmaster, or pass the phone to a friend. A coach explains every move in plain words, lines of light show what a piece can do, and the board brightens on the side that is ahead.

Support

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Questions

Do I need an account or the internet? No. Everything runs on your iPhone, including the engine.

How do I move a piece? Tap it, then tap where it should go, or drag it there.

What does a long press do? It shows which of the pieces guard the one you are pressing and which attack it.

How do I look back at earlier moves? Tap a move under the coach card, or swipe the card left and right. Tap the board to come back.

Where is my game saved? On your phone. Leave any time and it is where you left it.
