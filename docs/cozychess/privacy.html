Privacy policy

Calm Chess collects nothing.

Calm Chess has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not connect to the internet. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

  • The game in progress, so you can pick it up later.
  • Your recent results and accuracy, shown on the home screen.
  • Your sound and haptics settings.

These live only in the app's own storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app does.
